About

From enzyme kinetics to immune-cell function, I’ve followed the biology toward the clinic.

I started in protein biochemistry at IIT Madras, purifying recombinant xylose reductase and measuring its cofactor kinetics. At Northeastern I moved into biomaterials for nerve regeneration, then built CRISPR-edited Xenopus disease models at Unravel Biosciences.

Most recently, at Seismic Therapeutic, I ran PBMC, T-cell and immunogenicity assays for clinical-stage autoimmune programs, designing 9-color flow panels and analyzing them end to end. Today I teach the techniques I use, as a TA for Northeastern’s biotechnology lab course.
